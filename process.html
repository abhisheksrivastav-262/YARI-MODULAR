Step-By-Step Journey

The 5-Phase Turnkey Process

A transparent, predictable workflow built around your schedule and aesthetic expectations.

01

Discover

Understand your lifestyle, needs and vision.
We begin with an in-depth consultation at your space or our studio to analyze spatial dimensions, daily routines, storage priorities, and aesthetic aspirations.

  • Comprehensive site laser measurement & architectural audit
  • In-depth lifestyle interview & budget alignment session
  • Initial spatial layout zoning & flow recommendations
02

Concept

Develop the design direction, materials and aesthetic.
Our lead designers translate your brief into moodboards, color palettes, material samples (veneers, quartz, laminates, fabrics), and preliminary 2D layouts.

  • Custom tactile moodboard creation with physical material swatches
  • Color psychology & architectural lighting direction
  • Preliminary cost estimation & scope finalizing
03

Design

Transform the concept into detailed interior solutions.
We render photo-realistic 3D walkthroughs of every room, complete with exact furniture placements, lighting warmth, and millwork details, backed by millimeter-precise 2D working drawings.

  • 360-Degree photorealistic 3D renders for complete clarity
  • Detailed electrical, plumbing, false ceiling & elevation blueprints
  • Final itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ) with zero surprise costs
04

Build

Execute with attention to craftsmanship and detail.
Modular factory manufacturing runs in parallel with on-site civil, false ceiling, and electrical preparations under the constant supervision of a dedicated project manager.

  • State-of-the-art automated CNC modular cabinet fabrication
  • Strict quality audits for edge banding & anti-swell moisture resistance
  • Weekly digital photo progress updates provided to you
05

Handover

Deliver a finished space designed to be loved and lived in.
Following deep post-construction cleaning, snags resolution, and hardware fine-tuning, we welcome you to step into your impeccably crafted home.

  • Comprehensive 45-point quality checklist snag resolution
  • Professional deep cleaning & soft-furnishing staging
  • Formal keys handover with warranty certificates & care guide
